Assumptions used in this calculator

  • EBM bottles are single-use: one bottle can cover one feed only. Any leftover EBM is wasted.
  • PDBM from one bottle can be split across multiple feeds/babies, but a feed cannot combine PDBM from two bottles.
  • The algorithm aims to minimise waste first, then minimise bottle count. For small numbers of PDBM feeds it uses an exact search; for larger numbers it uses a heuristic with splitting into 50 ml bottles when possible.

Important reminders

  • Do not mix milk from different PDBM bottles in the same feed.
  • Always aim to minimise PDBM waste.
  • If any feed needs more than 125 ml of PDBM, the allocation will require clinical adjustment.
